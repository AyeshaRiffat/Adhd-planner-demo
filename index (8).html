How to Use

Everything you need to set up and get the most out of Adhd Planner. Tap any section to expand it.

👋 New here? Start with Getting Started below, then explore each tab in the sidebar in whatever order feels useful. Nothing here is required — Adhd Planner adapts to however you want to use it.
🚀 Getting Started ▸
The first time you open Adhd Planner, it will ask for your name — this is just a local profile so the app can greet you by name. It is not a cloud account: no email, no password, no server.
  • All your data (tasks, goals, habits, budget, notes, moods, calendar) is saved only in this browser, on this device, using your browser's local storage.
  • Nothing is uploaded anywhere — Adhd Planner works even without an internet connection (after the first load).
  • Your data stays saved between sessions automatically. Close the tab, shut down your device, come back next week — everything will still be there.
  • To use Adhd Planner on a different device or browser, use Backup & Restore in Settings (see below) to move your data over.
🔋 Brain Battery Home ▸
A quick daily energy check-in, found at the top of the Home tab.
  • Tap Low, Medium, or High each morning based on how much capacity you actually have that day.
  • Adhd Planner shows you a short, realistic note based on your choice — no guilt-tripping, just honest pacing advice.
  • It automatically resets every day, so you check in fresh each morning.
  • This doesn't lock any features — it's just a mindset tool to help you plan realistically instead of overcommitting.
☑ Tasks ▸
  • Type a task and pick a priority (High / Medium / Low), then tap Add.
  • Tap the ★ star icon on up to 3 tasks to pin them as "Today's Plan" — they'll also show up on your Home dashboard. The 3-task limit is intentional, to keep each day realistic.
  • Tap directly on a task's text to rename it — it becomes editable right there.
  • Tap the checkbox to mark a task done (it moves to the bottom, crossed out).
  • Tap ✕ to delete a task permanently.
◎ Goals ▸
For bigger, ongoing goals that aren't a single task — anything you're working toward over time.
  • Add a goal name, a target number, and an optional unit (e.g. "books", "kg", "sessions", "$").
  • Use the + and − buttons to update your progress whenever you make headway.
  • The progress bar and percentage update automatically.
  • Tap a goal's name to rename it, or ✕ to remove it.
↻ Habits ▸
  • Add any habit you want to track daily (e.g. "Drink water", "Take meds", "Stretch").
  • Tap the circle each day you complete it. Your streak (consecutive days) is shown next to each habit.
  • Streaks are real — if you miss a day, the streak resets. It's not just a total count, so it reflects genuine consistency.
  • There's no limit on how long a streak can run.
  • See your last 7 days of consistency for every habit as a bar chart on the Analytics tab.
$ Budget, Debt & Savings ▸
Budget Tracker
  • Add a category (e.g. "Groceries") with a budgeted amount.
  • Use + / − to log spending as it happens. The bar turns yellow near your limit, red if you go over.
Debt Payoff Calculator
  • Add each debt with its balance, APR (interest rate), and minimum payment.
  • Choose a strategy: Avalanche (pay off highest interest rate first — saves the most money) or Snowball (pay off smallest balance first — quicker wins for motivation).
  • Enter any extra monthly amount you can put toward debt beyond the minimums — Adhd Planner calculates a realistic payoff timeline and estimated total interest.
Savings Goals
  • Add a goal name and target amount (e.g. "Emergency Fund — $2,000").
  • Use + / − to log savings as you set money aside — the ring fills up as you get closer.
▦ Planner (Calendar) ▸
  • Toggle between Monthly and Weekly views at the top.
  • Monthly view: tap any day to add an event. Colored dots show what's scheduled that day, categorized by Work / Personal / Health / Finance / Other.
  • Weekly view: tap any hour slot on any day to add a timed event — this creates a visual time-block on your calendar.
  • Mark an event as a Bill when adding it — it'll automatically appear in the "Upcoming bills" list at the top of the Planner tab as a reminder.
  • Tap any event block to delete it.
✎ Brain Dump ▸
A no-structure space for whatever's in your head right now.
  • Tap + New sticky note to add one, then just start typing — it saves automatically as you type.
  • No folders, no categories, no setup — built for the moment you need to write something down immediately.
  • Tap ✕ on a note to delete it.
☺ Reflection ▸
  • Pick an emoji that matches your mood, optionally add a one-line note, then tap Log it.
  • Past entries are listed below — tap any entry's text to edit it, or ✕ to delete it.
  • Your last 7 entries appear as a mood trend chart on the Analytics tab, so you can spot patterns over time.
◧ Analytics ▸
A one-glance overview pulling together everything you've logged:
  • Today's task completion ring (based on your starred top 3)
  • 7-day habit consistency bars for every habit
  • 7-entry mood trend chart
  • Budget overview (spent vs. budgeted per category)
  • Savings goals overview
Everything here updates automatically — there's nothing to build or maintain manually.
⚙ Settings ▸
Theme — choose from 6 color themes (including a Dark Mode). Tap any swatch to apply it instantly; your choice is remembered.

Profile — "Switch profile" clears just your display name (not your data) so someone else's name can be entered.

Backup & Restore — this is the most important one if you use Adhd Planner on more than one device:
  • Tap ⬇ Download backup to save a `.json` file containing everything — tasks, goals, habits, budget, calendar, notes, moods.
  • To bring that data into Adhd Planner on another browser or device, open Adhd Planner there and tap ⬆ Restore from backup, then select the file.
  • Do this regularly, or any time before clearing your browser data, switching browsers, or getting a new device.
Data — "Reset all data" permanently erases everything on this device. Download a backup first if there's any chance you'll want it back.
💡 Tips for staying consistent ▸
  • Check in with your Brain Battery before anything else each day — it makes the rest of your planning more realistic.
  • Only star 3 tasks at a time. Resist the urge to star more — the limit is the feature.
  • If a habit streak breaks, just start again the next day. The streak logic is honest on purpose — it's not meant to be a source of guilt.
  • Use Brain Dump the moment a thought interrupts you, so you don't lose it and don't have to hold it in your head.
  • Download a backup once a week or so — it takes 5 seconds and protects months of data.
